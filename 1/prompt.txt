## 2. What one training example is

One (run, target stop, prediction moment t0) triple (`trip/build.py:1-10`, `EVALUATION.md:10-15`).

- **Entity:** one train run on one day = `id` minus trailing stop number (`build.py:67`).
- **t0:** the change-time of a randomly chosen already-departed stop *j* before the target (`build.py:132-141, 161-167`); with probability 200/1000 the example is *pre-departure*, t0 placed 0–120 min before the first planned departure (`build.py:42-43, 157, 164`). 23% pre-departure (`DESIGN.md:350-352`).
- **Target stop k:** one random stop with a planned arrival per run (`build.py:120-130`).
- **Target:** arrival delay in whole minutes, `round((arrival_change_time − arrival_planned_time)/60)`, clipped 0…999, or `cancelled` from the cancel flags (`build.py:78, 557-558`; `DESIGN.md:334-341` — `delay_in_min` is the *departure* delay, so it is **not** the label).
- **Answer format:** a single integer minute token, or the literal `cancelled` (`trip/prompts.py:13, 165-167, 262-263`); Llama 3.2 tokenises 0–999 as one token (`DESIGN.md:875-878`).
- **Horizon/lead:** minutes from t0 to planned arrival (`build.py:546`); buckets ≤15, 15–30, 30–60, 60–120, &gt;120 (`EVALUATION.md:51-52`); 4.4% of examples have negative lead (train already past planned arrival) (`DESIGN.md:363-364`).
